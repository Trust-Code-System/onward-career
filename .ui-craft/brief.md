## 1. Product purpose

Present a paid career support service that rewrites CVs, applies to suitable jobs on behalf of clients, and prepares them for interviews.

## 2. Primary user

A professional evaluating hands-on job-search support from their phone or laptop, who needs to understand the scope and choose a suitable package.

## 3. Three to five principles

1. Human support is the offer: describe the expert work before any platform features.
2. Show the work without promising an offer: no invented testimonials, affiliations, guarantees, or metrics.
3. Make ambition feel approachable: editorial character and real people rather than corporate stock layouts.
4. Give each section a different job: services, process, visibility, packages, and questions have distinct compositions.
5. Quality includes usable motion: fast controls, keyboard operation, reduced-motion support, and readable mobile layouts.

## 4. Success metric for the surface

A visitor can identify the three core services, compare package scope, and explore an intake-call preview. Reviewers can assess the complete responsive landing page before business details and backend integrations are added.

## 5. Out of scope

- Real booking, payments, contract signing, customer login, CV uploads, or application sending.
- Claims about business registration, licences, client outcomes, partnerships, or team size.
- Final prices, currency, customer targeting, legal policy drafting, and live deployment.

## 6. Learned constraints

- 2026-10-08: User explicitly delegates brand, font, tooling, and layout decisions and asks for a distinctive polished landing page first.
- 2026-10-08: Working name Onward. All visual application details are labelled illustrative. Avoid invented endorsements and prices.
- 2026-10-08: Single light-mode marketing surface with intentional dark sections; a theme switch is out of scope.
