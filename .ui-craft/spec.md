## Surface: landing

**Craft Read:** Marketing landing for professionals seeking career backup; ivory/cobalt editorial, variance 8, signature staggered career collage beneath the headline.

### Content inventory

Navigation; hero and human imagery; scope strip; asymmetric services with interactive CV comparison; three-step dark process; illustrative progress tracker; three package scopes without invented prices; accessible FAQs; final booking CTA; footer with preview disclosures.

### States

Mobile menu closed/open; service tab before/after; FAQ collapsed/expanded; intake modal closed, preferences, selected time, preview summary, validation error. Native dialog focus trapping, Escape, close button, and focus return. No remote mutation or simulated successful booking.

### Motion shape

Headline and subtext reveal in short sequence; collage stagger; section reveal once using IntersectionObserver. Interactive press feedback, fast state transitions. Reduced-motion users see all content immediately.

### Typography hierarchy

Hero Manrope with serif emphasis; upright Manrope section heads with restrained serif highlights; 16px body, 13px supporting labels, 12px tracked category labels used sparingly.

### Acceptance bar

- Three services and paid managed-search positioning clear.
- Responsive at 375, 768, and 1440px; no horizontal overflow.
- Working menu, service tabs, FAQs, package selection, and intake prototype.
- Visible focus, semantic headings, accessible dialog, reduced motion.
- No fabricated testimonials, client counts, job guarantees, affiliations, prices, or live booking claims.
- Images local and fonts locally hosted; build succeeds, browser console free of app errors.
- Actual desktop and mobile visual inspection; screenshot saved for review.

### Open questions (deferred to later phases)

Final name/logo/domain, package scopes/prices/currency, market, verified business data, contract, booking provider, payment provider, portal choice, testimonials, budget, deadline, maintenance.
